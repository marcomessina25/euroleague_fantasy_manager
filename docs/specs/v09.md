# V0.9 — Learned Availability Models, EuroCup Full Operational Parity & Cross-League Validation

> **Status:** Active on branch `v09` (created from `origin/main` after PR #13 merged V0.8).  
> **Planning baseline:** 2026-10-02  
> **Current active season:** 2026-27 (`E2026` for EuroLeague, `U2026` for EuroCup).  
> **Scope:** Regular season for both competitions (EuroLeague 34 rounds; EuroCup Groups A & B 18 rounds). *Playoffs restart fantasy from scratch with new rosters/rules and are disregarded.*  
> **Historical data scope:** 4 completed seasons (`2022-23`, `2023-24`, `2024-25`, `2025-26`) across both EuroLeague (`E`) and EuroCup (`U`).

---

## 1. Why V0.9 exists

V0.8 successfully introduced real historical box-score feeds, basketball context heuristics (blowouts, fouls, DRW congestion), injury usage redistribution, rank-aware portfolio presets, and option value.

However, several critical gaps remain before declaring production maturity in V1.0:
1. **Heuristic vs Learned Parameters**: V0.8's rotation and blowout penalties are rule-based heuristics. V0.9 trains scikit-learn models (`HistGradientBoostingClassifier`, `HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, and regularized Ridge/Logistic regression) on the 4 seasons of historical box scores to learn true non-linear participation and minutes surfaces without target leakage.
2. **EuroCup Operational Parity**: While V0.6 and V0.8 added EuroCup snapshot storage and feed ingestion, EuroCup's 2-group regular season structure (Group A and Group B, 10 clubs each, 18 rounds), bye weeks, and asymmetric schedules require first-class modeling in schedule analyzers, optimizers, and the workstation.
3. **Transfer Opportunity Cost Calibration**: With 4 free weekly trades, managers must balance burning trades for marginal upgrades vs preserving flexibility for double-round weeks (DRWs) and injury crises.
4. **Auditability & Error Attribution**: A formal ledger decomposing prediction error into model specification, managerial execution regret, and aleatoric basketball noise.

---

## 2. Core Principles & Invariants

1. **Shared Engine, Zero Competition Forks**: EuroLeague and EuroCup share 100% of data interfaces, storage schemas, team models, constraints, and optimization solvers.
2. **Strict Offline Test Invariant (F6)**: All test suites execute 100% offline using deterministic test fixtures and mock weights; no live network calls in automated test suites.
3. **Strict Zero-Leakage Point-in-Time Learning**: Features for round $R$ must only use information available before round $R$'s cutoff. Target variables from round $R$ are never visible during feature extraction.
4. **Regular Season Focus**: EuroLeague (34 rounds) and EuroCup (18 rounds) regular seasons are modeled. Postseason tournaments restart from scratch in official fantasy rules and are out of scope.
5. **Deterministic Fallback**: If scikit-learn model weights are missing or unseeded, the system gracefully falls back to V0.8 contextual decomposed projections (`fp_context_v08`).

## 4. Workstream Specifications

### 4.1 W1 — Learned Availability & Minutes Models (scikit-learn)
- **Feature Pipeline**: Extracts strictly point-in-time feature vectors from historical box scores:
  - Role stability: starter rate (last 5, season-to-date), DNP count (last 3), minutes trend (last 3 vs season mean).
  - Physical & discipline: foul rate per minute, foul drawn rate.
  - Team & opponent: team strength, opponent strength, win margin spread proxy, home/away advantage, double-round week flag.
- **Participation Classifier ($P(\text{play})$)**:
  - Scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (with logistic regression baseline) predicting probability of active court participation ($>0$ seconds).
  - Probability calibration via isotonic regression or Platt scaling.
- **Minutes Regressor ($\mathbb{E}[\text{minutes} \mid \text{play}]$)**:
  - Scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` (with Ridge regression baseline) predicting expected minutes played, naturally capturing non-linear blowout and foul truncations.
- **Model Registry & Registration**: Register model `learned_v09` in `prediction/registry.py` alongside `fp_context_v08` and `fp_decomposed_v03`.

### 4.2 W2 — EuroCup Full Operational Parity (Regular Season)
- **Dual-Group Structure**:
  - Group A: 10 clubs (18 regular season rounds).
  - Group B: 10 clubs (18 regular season rounds).
- **Asymmetric Schedules & Bye Weeks**:
  - Handle weeks where teams have byes or delayed fixtures; ensure players on bye project 0.0 FP with explicit `"bye"` status.
- **Club Quota Enforcement**:
  - Max 6 court players per club enforced uniformly across Groups A & B.
- **Head Coach Scoring in EuroCup**:
  - Head coach pricing and point rules ($10.0\text{ base} \pm \text{margin bonuses}$) validated for EuroCup clubs.

### 4.3 W3 — Dynamic Transfer Penalty & Turnover Calibration
- **Double-Round Week (DRW) Opportunity Cost**:
  - Model the trade expenditure policy: saving trades before a double-round week has higher option value due to heightened rotation volatility.
- **Liquidation Urgency Scoring**:
  - Prioritize liquidating confirmed injured or departed assets before speculative sideways upgrades.
- **Turnover Rate Tuning**:
  - Calibrate the transfer optimizer's net gain threshold to prevent burning trades for sub-0.5 FP speculative gains.

### 4.4 W4 — Error Attribution & Multi-Model Benchmark Ledger
- **Three-Way Telescoping Error Decomposition**:
  $$\text{Total Error} = \text{Model Error} + \text{Execution Regret} + \text{Aleatoric Noise}$$
  - *Model Error*: Discrepancy between model's expected score and an empirical post-hoc calibrated fit.
  - *Execution Regret*: Regret from suboptimal human transfers, captaincy, or bench ordering.
  - *Aleatoric Noise*: Irreducible basketball variance (in-game injuries, foul-outs, buzzer-beaters).
- **Multi-Model Benchmark Ledger**:
  - Automated benchmark comparing all model iterations across historical seasons:
    `season_mean` vs `last5` vs `xpdk_v02` vs `fp_decomposed_v03` vs `fp_context_v08` vs `learned_v09`.

### 4.5 W5 — Cross-League Validation Suite
- **Comprehensive Shared Engine Verification**:
  - Single test suite validating identical behavior for both EuroLeague (`league=euroleague`) and EuroCup (`league=eurocup`):
    - Ingestion and snapshot isolation.
    - Squad import and legality checking.
    - Single-round and multi-round transfer optimization.
    - Lineup optimization and formation legality.
    - Sequential decision replay.
    - Copilot dossier generation.
- **Zero Cross-League Bleed**:
  - Ensure zero shared state or ID collision between EuroLeague and EuroCup SQLite tables.

### 4.6 W6 — Web Workstation & CLI Integration
- **Workstation Competition Switcher**:
  - Seamless header toggle between EuroLeague and EuroCup teams.
  - Standings and group indicators (Group A / Group B for EuroCup).
- **Learned Feature Attribution in Player Intel Drawer**:

    e.g. `Base 24.2m | +1.8m (Home) | -2.1m (Blowout Risk) | -1.4m (Foul Risk) -> Final 22.5m`.
- **CLI Commands**:
