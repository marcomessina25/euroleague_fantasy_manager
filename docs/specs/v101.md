# V1.0.1 Specification: Mid-Season Squad Creation from Specific Round

**Target Version:** `1.0.1`  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Scope:** Core Domain, Multi-Team Management, Optimization Service, Decision Audit, CLI, and Workstation GUI  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

In the official EuroLeague (and EuroCup) Fantasy Challenge, managers join or begin utilizing algorithmic decision support at arbitrary points during the active season (e.g., Round 5, Round 15, or the current live round).

Previously, squads could only be drafted under Round 1 constraints:
1. **Fixed Starting Budget:** Squad cost was strictly capped at 100.0 credits (1,000 tenths), with bank credits automatically calculated as $100.0 - \text{squad\_cost}$.
2. **Historical Dependency:** To manage a team starting at a later round, the user had to simulate or backfill the entire historical sequence of transactions and substitutions from Round 1 forward.

However, mid-season fantasy squads naturally diverge from 100.0 credits:
- Player market values fluctuate by up to $\pm 15\%$ after every round based on actual performance.
- A manager's active 11-unit squad might be worth e.g. 104.2 credits, with 1.8 credits remaining in the bank (total spending power: 106.0 credits).
- Attempting to draft this squad at Round 5 under Round 1 constraints would fail with `Squad total cost exceeds allowed budget 100.0 Cr`.

**V1.0.1** introduces native **Mid-Season Squad Creation from a Specific Round**:
- Managers can initialize an isolated team profile starting at any round $R \ge 1$ (including the active live round).
- For $R = 1$, the classic fixed starting budget of 100.0 credits is enforced.
- For $R > 1$, the user enters their current bank credits (or total team value). Squad legality is verified across positions (4 Guards, 4 Forwards, 2 Centers, 1 Head Coach), unique players, and competition club limits ($\le 6$ court players per club) without capping squad market cost at 100.0 credits.
- The team's active pointer, squad baseline, and round checkpoint are immediately established for round $R$.
- Point-in-time transfer optimization and lineup recommendations are immediately available for round $R$ without requiring any past transaction history.

## 2. Architecture & Service Contracts

### 2.1 Multi-Team & CRUD Layer (`TeamService` & `routes_teams.py`)

#### API Endpoint: `POST /api/teams`
```json
{
  "name": "Panathinaikos Elite",
  "league": "euroleague",
  "season": "2026/27",
  "round_number": 5,
  "credits": 1.5,
  "player_ids": [101, 102, 103, 104, 105, 106, 107, 108, 109, 110, 111]
}
```

#### Rules & Invariants:
1. **Round Validation:** `round_number >= 1`.
2. **Credits Requirement:**
   - When `round_number == 1`: Credits are optional and default to $100.0 - \sum \text{price}$. Budget cap of 100.0 Cr is strictly enforced via `validate_squad_constraints(contracts, budget_tenths=1000)`.
   - When `round_number > 1`: Bank credits (`credits` or `bank_tenths`) must be explicitly specified (HTTP 400 if omitted). Bank credits cannot be negative ($bank \ge 0$).
3. **Mid-Season Squad Validation:**
   - Squad constraints are verified using `validate_squad_constraints(contracts, check_budget=False)`.
   - Roster quotas (4 G, 4 F, 2 C, 1 HC) and club quotas ($\le 6$ court players from the same club) are enforced.
4. **Point-in-Time Baseline Checkpoint:**
   - `save_round_checkpoint` records the initial state directly at `round_number=R`.
   - Decision audit logs the initial team decision with `round_number=R`.

### 2.2 Optimization Service (`OptimizationService`)
- `suggest_initial_team(season, round_number=1, budget_credits=100.0, ...)` and `recommend_initial_team`:
  Now accept `round_number`. When $R > 1$, player projection contracts and market valuations are fetched for round $R$ rather than defaulting to Round 1.
- `optimize_transfers`:
  Takes the team's live squad at round $R$ and available bank credits. Available spending power is calculated as:
  $$\text{Budget}_{\text{trade}} = \text{Bank} + \sum_{p \in \text{Transfers Out}} \text{Price}(p, R)$$
  No prior round transactions or checkpoints from rounds $1 \dots R-1$ are needed.
- Transfer screening enforces the full transfer legality rules (position quotas, unique players, budget, and the $\le 6$ court-players-per-club quota) before ranking. Exact lineup re-optimization runs on the top $\max(25, \text{top\_n})$ screened packages. The workstation requests `top_n = 30` (API range 1–50); other callers default to 5.

### 2.3 Live-Round Roll-Forward
Reading a team never changes it. Teams are rolled forward only when competition data is updated (`elf update` or `POST /api/workstation/update-data`): every managed team of that league whose round is behind the latest snapshot round has its squad carried forward unchanged to the live round, with current prices refreshed from the snapshot and `transfers_remaining` reset to 4. The new round's start checkpoint records this state. A team that fails to roll forward is reported (CLI stderr warning, `sync_failures` in the API response, GUI alert) and does not block other teams.

### 2.4 Workstation Endpoints (`routes_workstation.py`)
- `GET /api/workstation/current-round?league=euroleague`:
  Returns the active current round number for the selected competition from the snapshot store.
- `POST /api/workstation/initial-team/suggest`:
  Accepts `round_number: int = 1` and `budget_credits: float = 100.0`.

## 3. CLI Integration

### 3.1 Creating a Team Mid-Season
```bash
# Create team at round 5 with 1.5 bank credits
elf team create --id team_5 --name "Mid-Season Squad" --round 5 --credits 1.5

# Create team at round 5 with 1.5 bank credits and squad imported from players.txt
elf team create --id team_5 --name "Mid-Season Squad" --round 5 --credits 1.5 --file players.txt
```

### 3.2 Importing a Squad into an Existing Team
```bash
elf team import-squad --team team_5 --round 5 --credits 1.5 --file players.txt
```

`players.txt` holds one player or coach name per line (`#` starts a comment). Every line must resolve to exactly one player in the team's competition, otherwise the command fails and lists the unresolved names. `--players 101,102,...` (IDs) and `--file` are mutually exclusive, as are `--credits` and `--bank` (tenths). In round 1, omitting both gives `bank = 100.0 - squad cost`.

### 3.3 Offline Squad Import (`import-squad`)
```bash
elf import-squad --round 5 --credits 1.5 --file players.txt --squad config/current_squad.json
```

## 4. Workstation GUI (`index.html` & `app.js`)

In the **🏀 Initial Team Builder** modal:
1. **Starting Round Input:** A dedicated `Round` input allows selecting any round from 1 to 38 (defaults automatically to the competition's current live round).
2. **Dynamic Bank Credits:**
   - When `Round == 1`: "Bank" is automatically calculated as $100.0 - \text{Spent}$ under the 100.0 Cr salary cap.
   - When `Round > 1`: "Bank Cr *" becomes an active input field. The modal header displays **Total Team Value** ($\text{Squad Value} + \text{Bank Cr}$).
3. **Round-Scoped Player Universe:** Changing the round reloads player valuations and projections for that specific matchday.
4. **Suggest Squad:** Solves optimal initial draft using that round's expected fantasy points and selected budget.
