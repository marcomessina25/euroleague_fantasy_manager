## 1. Executive objective

The primary objective of V0.2 is:

> **Transform the deterministic V0.1 rules engine into a complete weekly EuroLeague Fantasy decision-support engine capable of evaluating squad financials, ranking multi-round fixtures, projecting player and Head Coach fantasy points ($\text{xPDK}$), optimizing Turn-1 / Turn-2 court lineups and captaincy via Real Option Value, and recommending legal 1-to-4 trade moves.**

```text
V0.1 Foundation (SQLite snapshots + deterministic rules + trade validator)
        ↓
V0.2 Workstreams:
  ├── P1: Detailed Squad & Capital-Gain Financial Report (`elf squad`)
  ├── P2: Position-Aware Matchup Difficulty (FDR) & Multi-Round Ticker (`elf fixtures`)
  ├── P3: Heuristic Baseline Player (PIR + 10% Win Bonus) & Head Coach Margin xPDK (`expected_points.py`)
  ├── P4: Turn-Aware Lineup, 6th-Man, Bench & Captain Optimizer (`elf lineup`)
  └── P5: Legal 1-to-4 Trade Candidate Generator & Ranker (`elf suggest-trades`)
```

In EuroLeague Fantasy Classic Mode, a manager's weekly workflow is governed by four mechanics that differ fundamentally from soccer fantasy (FPL):

1. **Zero Sell-On Tax (`100%` Capital Gains)**: Selling a player who rose from `9.5 Cr` to `12.0 Cr` yields the full `12.0 Cr` in purchasing power. Tracking purchase price vs. live quotation (`total_plus`) is essential for squad valuation.
2. **Weighted 11-Unit Scoring (`5 Starters @ 1.0x`, `Captain @ 2.0x`, `6th Man @ 1.0x`, `4 Bench @ 0.5x`, `Head Coach @ 1.0x`)**: Every single unit on the 11-man roster contributes points every round—there are no `0.0x` dead bench slots.
3. **Multi-Turn Intra-Round Substitutions (`Turn 1 -> Turn 2`)**: A Round spans 2–3 days (`Turn 1` on Tuesday/Thursday, `Turn 2` on Wednesday/Friday). Starting a `Turn 1` player while keeping a `Turn 2` player on the `0.5x` bench creates a **Real Option**: if the `Turn 1` starter underperforms, you can substitute the `Turn 2` bench player into the `1.0x` slot (or switch your `2.0x` Captaincy from `Turn 1` to `Turn 2`).
4. **Up to 4 Free Trades Between Rounds (Including Head Coach)**: Managers can execute `1..4` trades between rounds (or unlimited trades during the 6 Unlimited Windows: `after R6, R13, R18, R23, R28, R34`).

## 3. Mathematical Specifications & Heuristic Model Contract

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **V0.2 Model Contract (Heuristic Decision-Support Baseline)**:
> - V0.2 `xPDK` is an **intentionally heuristic decision-support baseline**, **not** a machine-learning model or a statistically fitted / historically validated forecast.
> - Official market `quotation` (`credits`) is intentionally blended as an input prior alongside per-game fantasy scoring so the engine remains well-behaved in Round 1–4 before large sample sizes accumulate.
> - Player score standard deviation ($\sigma_i = \max(4.0, 0.45 \cdot \text{xPDK}_i)$) is a **heuristic uncertainty estimate** used for Turn 1 $\to$ Turn 2 option-value calculations, not empirical player game-to-game variance.
> - This V0.2 heuristic baseline is preserved as a deterministic benchmark so **V0.2.5** ([`docs/v025/v025.md`](../v025/v025.md)) can evaluate it out-of-sample against historical baselines before **V0.3** ([`docs/v03/v03.md`](../v03/v03.md)).

### 3.1 Squad Financial & Capital-Gain Dossier (`P1: squad_report.py`)

For each of the 11 units $i \in \{1, \dots, 11\}$ in `config/current_squad.json`:
- **Purchase Price ($P_{\text{buy}, i}$)**: Stored in tenths of a Credit (`purchase_prices_tenths[i]`).
- **Market Quotation ($P_{\text{mkt}, i}$)**: Live `price_tenths` from the latest SQLite snapshot.
- **Selling Value ($P_{\text{sell}, i}$)**: Under EuroLeague Fantasy rules (`0%` sell-on tax), $P_{\text{sell}, i} = P_{\text{mkt}, i}$.
- **Unrealized Capital Gain ($\Delta \text{Cr}_i$)**:
  $$\Delta \text{Cr}_i = \frac{P_{\text{mkt}, i} - P_{\text{buy}, i}}{10.0}$$
- **Total Liquid Team Value ($V_{\text{team}}$)**:
  $$V_{\text{team}} = \frac{\text{Bank}_{\text{tenths}} + \sum_{i=1}^{11} P_{\text{sell}, i}}{10.0}$$

`elf squad` reports per-unit purchase price, current quotation, net capital gain (`+Cr` / `-Cr`), club concentration (`x / 6`), position distribution (`4G, 4F, 2C, 1HC`), available free trades (`0..4`), and next Unlimited Trade Window (`R7, R14, R19, R24, R29, R35`).

### 3.2 Multi-Round Fixture Ticker & Position-Specific FDR (`P2: fixtures.py`)

Each EuroLeague Round consists of **Turns (`T1`, `T2`, `T3`)** corresponding to game days (e.g. Tuesday vs Wednesday in double-round weeks, or Thursday vs Friday in single-round weeks). All quantities below are intentionally heuristic in V0.2:

1. **Heuristic Team Strength Index ($S_t \in [0.30, 0.85]$)**:
   - Computed from each team's top-8 court player quotations (`price_tenths`) plus $1.5 \times$ Head Coach `price_tenths`:
     $$\text{Raw}_t = \sum_{k=1}^{8} P_{\text{top8}, t, k} + 1.5 \cdot P_{\text{HC}, t}, \quad S_t = 0.30 + 0.55 \cdot \frac{\text{Raw}_t - \min(\text{Raw})}{\max(1.0, \max(\text{Raw}) - \min(\text{Raw}))}$$
2. **Heuristic Win Probability & Expected Margin ($\mu_{\Delta}, \sigma_{\Delta}$)**:
   - For team $A$ vs opponent $B$ with home-court advantage $H = +3.8$ points (`-3.8` away), spread scale $\alpha = 26.0$, and margin standard deviation $\sigma_{\Delta} = 11.5\text{ pts}$:
     $$\mu_{\Delta} = 26.0 \cdot (S_A - S_B) \pm 3.8, \quad P(\text{Win}_A) = \Phi\left(\frac{\mu_{\Delta}}{11.5}\right)$$
3. **Fixture Difficulty Rating ($\text{FDR} \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$) & Position Multipliers ($\lambda_{\text{FDR}}$)**:
   - Maps $\text{DifficultyScore} = S_{\text{opp}} + (0.00 \text{ if Home else } 0.12)$ into thresholds `< 0.42 -> 1`, `< 0.54 -> 2`, `< 0.66 -> 3`, `< 0.78 -> 4`, $\ge 0.78 \to 5$.
   - Position-specific heuristic matchup multiplier $\lambda_{\text{FDR}} \in [0.86, 1.14]$:
     $$\lambda_{\text{FDR}}(\text{FDR}, p, \text{is\_home}) = 1.0 + (3 - \text{FDR}) \cdot \beta_p + \begin{cases} +0.020 & \text{if Home} \\ -0.015 & \text{if Away} \end{cases}$$
     where $\beta_{\text{G}} = 0.045$, $\beta_{\text{F}} = 0.050$, $\beta_{\text{C}} = 0.055$, $\beta_{\text{HC}} = 0.060$.
4. **Turn Tagging (`T1`, `T2`, `T3`)**:
   - Every fixture cell in `elf fixtures --rounds 5` displays Opponent, Venue (`H`/`A`), Turn (`T1`/`T2`), and `FDR` (e.g., `R1:RMB(H,T1,FDR4)`).

### 3.3 Heuristic Baseline Expected Fantasy Points ($\text{xPDK}$) Model (`P3: expected_points.py`)

#### A. Court Players (`G`, `F`, `C`)
EuroLeague Fantasy player score is **Modified PIR + 10% Team Win Bonus**:
$$\text{Score}_i = \text{PIR}_i \times \left(1 + 0.10 \cdot \mathbb{I}(\text{Team}_i \text{ Wins})\right)$$

In the shipped V0.2 baseline (`project_player_for_round`), the heuristic components are:
- **Market-Price Input Prior ($\text{PricePrior}_i$)**:
  $$\text{PricePrior}_i = \text{Cr}_i \times \begin{cases} 1.02 & \text{if status} = \text{starter} \\ 0.78 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
- **Base PIR Blend ($\text{BasePIR}_i$)**:
  $$\text{BasePIR}_i = \begin{cases} 0.55 \cdot \text{AvgPDK}_i + 0.45 \cdot \text{PricePrior}_i & \text{if } \text{AvgPDK}_i > 0 \\ 0.35 \cdot \text{LastPDK}_i + 0.65 \cdot \text{PricePrior}_i & \text{else if } \text{LastPDK}_i > 0 \\ \text{PricePrior}_i & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
- **Heuristic Availability & Depth-Chart Multiplier ($A_i$)**:
  $$A_i = \begin{cases} 0.00 & \text{if injured / out / suspended or } P(\text{play}_i) \le 0 \\ P(\text{play}_i) \times 1.00 & \text{if status} = \text{starter} \\ P(\text{play}_i) \times 0.85 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
- **Matchup & Win-Bonus Multiplier**:
  $$\text{xPDK}_i = A_i \cdot \text{BasePIR}_i \cdot \lambda_{\text{FDR}}(\text{FDR}, \text{pos}_i, \text{is\_home}) \cdot \left(1 + 0.10 \cdot P(\text{Win}_{\text{team}_i})\right)$$
- **Heuristic Player Score Uncertainty ($\sigma_i$)**:
  $$\sigma_i = \begin{cases} \max\left(4.0, \, 0.45 \cdot \text{xPDK}_i\right) & \text{if } A_i > 0 \\ 0.0 & \text{if } A_i = 0 \end{cases}$$

#### B. Head Coaches (`HC`)
Head Coaches score discrete step points based on final margin $M = \text{Score}_{\text{team}} - \text{Score}_{\text{opp}}$:
- Win by $1\text{--}10$ (or OT): $+10$
- Win by $11\text{--}20$: $+20$
- Win by $21+$: $+25$
- Loss by $1\text{--}10$ (or OT): $-5$
- Loss by $11\text{--}20$: $-10$
- Loss by $21+$: $-20$

Using the normal margin distribution $M \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_{\Delta}, \sigma_{\Delta}^2)$:
$$\text{xPDK}_{\text{HC}} = +25 \cdot P(M > 20.5) + 20 \cdot P(10.5 < M \le 20.5) + 10 \cdot P(0 < M \le 10.5) - 5 \cdot P(-10.5 \le M < 0) - 10 \cdot P(-20.5 \le M < -10.5) - 20 \cdot P(M < -20.5)$$

### 3.4 Turn-Aware Lineup & Captaincy Optimizer (`P4: lineup.py`)

In Classic Mode, all 11 squad members contribute to the round total:
- **5 Starters**: $1.0\times$ (with **1 Captain** at $2.0\times$, i.e., $+1.0\times$ bonus)
- **1 Sixth Man**: $1.0\times$
- **4 Bench Players**: $0.5\times$
- **1 Head Coach**: $1.0\times$

Without intra-round substitutions, the static expected points of a lineup assignment is:
$$\text{xP}_{\text{static}} = \text{xPDK}_{\text{HC}} + \text{xPDK}_{\text{Cap}} + \sum_{i \in \text{Starters} \cup \{\text{6th}\}} \text{xPDK}_i + 0.5 \sum_{j \in \text{Bench}_4} \text{xPDK}_j$$

#### The Turn-1 $\to$ Turn-2 Real Option Value ($\Delta \text{Option xP}$)
When a `1.0x` slot (Starter or Sixth Man) is assigned to a **Turn 1 (`T1`)** player $i$ while an eligible **Turn 2 (`T2`)** player $j$ (capable of forming a legal formation after substitution) is placed on the `0.5x` Bench:
- After `T1` finishes, the manager observes the **exact realized score $S_i$**.
- If $S_i < \text{xPDK}_j$, the manager substitutes $j$ in ($1.0\times$) and demotes $i$ to the bench ($0.5\times$).
- If $S_i \ge \text{xPDK}_j$, the manager keeps $i$ at $1.0\times$ and leaves $j$ at $0.5\times$.
- The **Option Value gain** over static scoring for pair $(i \in T_1, j \in T_2)$ is:
  $$\Delta \text{Option}_{\text{slot}}(i, j) = 0.5 \cdot \mathbb{E}\left[\max\left(0, \, \text{xPDK}_j - S_i\right)\right]$$
  Under $S_i \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_i, \sigma_i^2)$, with $z = \frac{\mu_j - \mu_i}{\sigma_i}$:
  $$\mathbb{E}\left[\max(0, \mu_j - S_i)\right] = (\mu_j - \mu_i)\Phi(z) + \sigma_i \phi(z)$$
- Similarly, for **Captaincy (`2.0x`)**, assigning the `T1` armband to $c_1 \in T_1$ with a designated `T2` vice-captain $c_2 \in T_2$ yields an additional **Captain Option Value**:
  $$\Delta \text{Option}_{\text{Cap}}(c_1, c_2) = \mathbb{E}\left[\max\left(0, \, \mu_{c_2} - S_{c_1}\right)\right] = (\mu_{c_2} - \mu_{c_1})\Phi\left(\frac{\mu_{c_2} - \mu_{c_1}}{\sigma_{c_1}}\right) + \sigma_{c_1}\phi\left(\frac{\mu_{c_2} - \mu_{c_1}}{\sigma_{c_1}}\right)$$

`elf lineup` enumerates all legal assignments of the 10 court players into `(5 Starters in a legal formation, 1 Captain, 1 Sixth Man, 4 Bench)` + `1 Head Coach`, maximizes:
$$\text{Total Turn-Adjusted xPDK} = \text{xP}_{\text{static}} + \Delta \text{Option}_{\text{Cap}} + \Delta \text{Option}_{\text{slot}}$$
and passes the winning lineup through `rules.validate_court_lineup()` before returning it.

### 3.5 Automated 1-to-4 Trade Candidate Generator & Ranker (`P5: suggest_transfers.py`)

Given the manager's `CurrentSquadState` (`11` units, `bank_tenths`, `free_trades` $\in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$):
1. Pre-filters candidate incoming players/coaches per position (`G`, `F`, `C`, `HC`) using top raw `expected_pdk` and top value-per-credit (`expected_pdk / credits`).
2. Searches legal `k`-trade combinations ($k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, including Head Coach swaps) satisfying:
   - Position preservation (`4G, 4F, 2C, 1HC`)
   - Club limit ($\le 6$ court players per club)
   - Affordability ($\text{Bank}_{\text{before}} + \sum P_{\text{sell, out}} - \sum P_{\text{buy, in}} \ge 0$)
3. Uses a **two-stage marginal xPDK pre-filter** (`shortlist = max(25, top_k * 6)`) followed by full Turn-1 $\to$ Turn-2 lineup re-optimization (`optimize_court_lineup`) to compute the **net gain in Turn-Adjusted Roster xPDK ($\Delta \text{xPDK} > 0$)**. *(Note: this two-stage marginal filtering is a V0.2 runtime performance optimization to keep `1..4` trade searches under `1s`, not yet a formally proven exact search reduction for arbitrary nonlinear multi-unit objectives.)*
4. Verifies every recommended trade bundle via `transfers.validate_trades()` before surfacing it in `elf suggest-trades`.
